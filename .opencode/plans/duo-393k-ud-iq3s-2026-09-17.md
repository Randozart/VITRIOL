## Goal

Two simultaneous coding sessions on one engine. Shared kv-unified pool of
393,216 tokens (q4_0/q4_0), each session capped at 262,144 (n_ctx_train,
enforced by server-context.cpp n_ctx_slot()). Workflow: one session compacts
while the other keeps filling - joint 393K budget absorbs the overlap.

## Key insight (corrects 2026-09-16 estimate)

With kv-unified (llama-context.cpp:290) the KV pool is SHARED across slots,
sized by -c alone. --parallel does NOT multiply KV VRAM. Earlier "2x262K =
26.1 GiB" claim applied only to partitioned pools. A 393K shared pool costs
7.17 GiB regardless of slot count.
